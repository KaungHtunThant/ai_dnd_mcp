# The Three Walls

tags: world, walls, core

Humanity lives inside three concentric walls, each fifty meters high: Wall Maria (outermost), Wall Rose, and Wall Sina (innermost, home of the capital and the nobility). Beyond them roam Titans, giant humanoids that eat people without need or reason. The Survey Corps ventures outside, the Garrison guards the walls, and the Military Police keep order in the interior. All three use ODM gear (omni-directional mobility gear): gas-powered grapple harnesses that let soldiers fly through the air and cut the nape of a Titan's neck, the only reliable weak spot.
